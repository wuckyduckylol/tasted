tasted— ui polish & motion review
What I read: all 5 tab screens, chain / item / rate flows, theme.ts, ui.tsx, ItemRow, Marquee, StepScreen, and the get-started walkthrough. Tokens: coral #FF5A36, candy #F290B9, cream #FAF7F2, Baloo 2 + Nunito.

Diagnosis — why the app feels flatter than the sign-up: the walkthrough (1a) commits to the brand: candy field, confetti, 76px lowercase wordmark, ticker. Inside the app the brand shrinks to a thin pink header strip; every element becomes the same white card with border and shadow; system fonts leak in (stats, toggles, NEW badge, community block); radii drift (6/9/10/12/14); lists just appear — zero motion. And the app's hero promise — the best thing to order — never shows on home. Copy leaks dev-speak ("Run node supabase/seed/seed.mjs").

Below: 2 four home directions + the rating celebration + Nunito alternatives + palette riffs · 1 the current app, recreated faithfully with audit notes.

The celebration from 2e stays — 4d is the same moment in Figtree. Every screen replays as you scroll to it.
